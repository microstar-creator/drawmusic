Privacy Policy

Draw Music for Android · Effective date: 10 November 2026 · Last updated: 10 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Draw Music app (“the app”), made by MICROSTAR TECH LAB (“we”, “us”), handles information. It applies to the app and to any support we provide for it.

In short: Draw Music works entirely on your device. We have no accounts and no servers; the app uses Google services for subscriptions, advertising and anonymous usage statistics (see sections 5 and 5a). Your drawings, music, exported files and the photos you choose stay on your phone and are never sent to these services. We do not sell your personal information.

2. Information the app handles

The app only handles information that you create or choose inside it. It keeps all of it in the app’s private storage on your device.

3. Permissions and system features

  • Photos: “Add Your Photo” uses the Android system photo picker. The app only receives the single photo you select; it cannot see the rest of your gallery and does not ask for photo or storage permission for this.
  • Storage (Android 8 and 9 only): when you tap “Save” on an exported file, these older Android versions require the storage permission to place the file in your Download/DrawMusic folder. On Android 10 and later no permission is needed.
  • Internet: the app’s own features never use the internet. The internet permission is used by Google Play Billing (Premium), Google AdMob (ads) and Google Analytics for Firebase (usage statistics); see sections 5 and 5a. The advertising ID permission is used by AdMob and Firebase.
  • The app does not use the camera, the microphone, your location or your contacts.

4. Sharing your information

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone, because it never leaves your device through us.

Your files leave the app only when you choose so:

  • Save: copies an exported file to your device’s Download/DrawMusic folder, where other apps on your phone (for example your gallery or file manager) can see it.
  • Open with another app: when you tap an exported file, Android lets you choose an app to open it. That app then handles the file under its own privacy policy.

5. Purchases, ratings and advertising

The free version of the app shows advertising (see section 5a). Premium users see no ads.

Premium subscription: purchases are made and paid through Google Play under Google’s terms and the Google Privacy Policy. We never see your name, email or payment details. The app only receives from Google Play, on your device, which Premium plan is active and a purchase token, so it can unlock Premium. You manage or cancel the subscription in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions.

Google Play Billing Library: to sell Premium, the app includes this component from Google. Google states that it may send Google technical diagnostic information about how the billing component works (for example errors and response times), handled under the Google Privacy Policy. It does not send your drawings, music or photos.

5a. Advertising and usage statistics

Google AdMob: the free version shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner under the drawing tools, ads you choose to watch for a reward, and occasionally a full-screen ad after an export. AdMob may collect the advertising ID, device and network information, approximate location from the IP address, and ad interactions, to show, measure and (where you allow it) personalize ads and to prevent fraud. See How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Consent: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and other places where it is required, the app asks for your choice through Google’s consent message before ads are requested, and you can change it later. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone’s settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads).

Google Analytics for Firebase: the app sends anonymous usage events (for example that a first drawing was made, that a song was played or exported, or that the Premium page was opened) with an app instance ID, device model, Android version, language and approximate country, so we can improve the app. These events never contain your drawings, music, photos or file names. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Rating: when you give 4 or 5 stars, the app asks Google Play to show its rating card. Your rating and review go to Google Play, not to us.

6. How long information is kept and how to delete it

  • Drafts stay on your device until you remove the app. Exported files stay until you delete them in the Library (⋮ → Delete) or remove the app.
  • Removing the app deletes all its private data (drafts, Library files, background photos). Files you saved to the Download folder stay there until you delete them yourself.
  • The app’s private data is excluded from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so it is not copied to backup services.

7. Security

Your information stays in the app’s private storage, which Android protects from other apps. The app’s own features do not send your information over the network. Please protect your device with a screen lock, since anyone who can use your unlocked phone can open the app.

8. Children

Draw Music is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. Because the free version shows ads and uses analytics (section 5a), we do not knowingly allow children under 13 to use it. The app has no chat, accounts or ways to share with other users inside the app. If you are a parent or guardian and have a question about your child’s use of the app, contact us at the email above.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the European Union and the United Kingdom, or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, and not to be discriminated against for using these rights. Because we do not collect or hold any of your personal information, we have nothing to give, correct or delete on our side; everything is on your device and under your control. You can still contact us with any request or question, and you may complain to your local data protection authority.

We do not sell or share personal information as these terms are defined in the CCPA/CPRA.

10. International transfers

Your drawings, music and photos are never transferred. Information collected by Google AdMob, Google Analytics for Firebase and Google Play may be processed by Google in other countries, including the United States, under Google’s own safeguards.

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above. For important changes, such as collecting a new kind of information, we will also tell you inside the app or in the store listing before the change applies.
